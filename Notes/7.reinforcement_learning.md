# Reinforcement learning

- the goal of reinforcement leanring is to train an agent to complete a task within an uncertain environment.

- the agent receives observations and a reward from the environment and sends actions to the environment.

- the reward measures how succesful action is with respect to completing the task goal. 


for example: learning how to ride a bicycle -



here you take the bike and learn on your own how to ride the bicycle.

agent - is you
action - is provided by the enviroment


goal of agent - maximize the reward. i.e more positive feedback, better driving

enviroment - is providing him a reward, ie. feedback
